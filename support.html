How do I play?

Read a dossier. Tap words with a dotted red underline to file them in your Excerpt Book. Where a word is lost under an ink stain, tap the stain and restore it with the right excerpt — the answer is always somewhere else in the archive or in the Chronicle.

I'm stuck

Get one answer wrong and the Old Archivist gives you a hint. Get two wrong and a button takes you straight to where the clue is. Your Excerpt Book (top right on the Investigate tab) lists every open clue with its hint.

How do I change the language?

The app follows your iPhone's language. To use a different language for this app only: Settings → Spectre Files → Language.

How do I start over?

Me → Restart Investigation. Your member card and bookmarks are kept.

How do I turn off sounds?

Me → Sound Effects. The app's sounds also follow the silent switch.

Restoring purchases

If you became a Supporting Member on another device, open Me → Support the Society and tap Restore Purchases.

About the content

The archive is compiled by history enthusiasts, not an official or academic institution, and is not affiliated with any party or government. Facts and interpretation are kept apart, and every dossier lists its sources.

怎么玩？

阅读卷宗，点击带红色虚线的词，把它收进摘录本。被墨迹盖住的字，点一下墨迹，用对的摘录把它复原——答案总在档案室的别处或年表里。

卡住了怎么办？

答错一次，老档案员会给提示；答错两次，会出现直达线索所在处的按钮。「调查」页右上角的摘录本里，列着所有待复原的线索和提示。

怎么切换语言？

App 跟随 iPhone 的系统语言。只想让这个 App 用别的语言：设置 → 幽灵档案 → 语言。

怎么重新开始？

我的 → 重新开始调查。会员证和收藏会保留。

怎么关闭音效？

我的 → 音效。App 的声音也跟随静音键。

恢复购买

在另一台设备上成为过赞助会员：打开「我的 → 支持同好会」，点「恢复购买」。

关于内容

档案由一群历史爱好者整理，非官方、非学术机构，与任何政党或政府无关。史实与观点分开标注，每份卷宗都列出参考来源。
